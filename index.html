Investing · Activation

Upstox: First-Trade Activation

Why do users who finish KYC never place a first trade?

Read case study ↗
Problem
Account opening and KYC cost the business real money, but a big share of verified users never make a first investment, so that spend never turns into revenue.
Insight
New investors land on chart-heavy, F&O-first screens built for active traders. Adding funds takes several steps, and there is no guided path to a safe first investment.
Solution
A beginner mode with a guided starter SIP in an index fund or ETF, plus one-tap UPI fund-add from the same screen. Prioritized with RICE against the other options.
Metrics
North star: KYC-to-first-trade conversion. Supporting: D30 activation. Guardrail: beginner users moving into F&O too early.
Lending · Conversion

Moneyview: Loan Application Drop-off

Where does the funnel from eligibility to disbursal leak?

Read case study ↗
Problem
Users who are already eligible for a personal loan abandon the application before disbursal. These are the highest-intent users in the funnel.
Insight
Drop-off peaks at bank-statement upload and e-mandate setup. Users also hesitate because they can't see the final EMI and fees until late in the flow.
Solution
Account Aggregator consent instead of manual uploads, save-and-resume with a progress bar, and an upfront EMI and fee summary in line with Key Fact Statement norms.
Metrics
North star: application-to-disbursal rate. Supporting: time-to-disbursal. Guardrail: early-default rate must not rise as friction falls.
B2B Spend · Adoption

Zaggle: Expense Card Adoption

Why do employees under-use corporate cards while approvals pile up?

Read case study ↗
Problem
An SMB buys the platform, but employees keep paying personally and claiming later, and managers clear approvals in batches. The finance team doesn't get the control it paid for.
Insight
Three users, three different frictions: employees find receipt capture tedious, managers approve in bulk, and finance deals with unclear categories at month-end close.
Solution
WhatsApp/photo receipt capture with OCR auto-categorization, plus policy-based auto-approval for low-value spends so managers only review the exceptions.
Metrics
North star: expense cycle time. Supporting: weekly active cardholders. Guardrail: out-of-policy spend that gets auto-approved.

The same loop runs through every project above.

Ask why
