Hi, I'm Sudiksha

I want to build money products people actually trust.

My path into product didn't start with a title. As a project coordinator, I kept asking why a feature was being built before asking how. Then I watched a manufacturing firm run its purchasing on paper and memory, so I built the system they now use every day. That was the first time I saw a product change how people work.

Fintech pulled me in because the stakes are personal. One confusing screen in a lending or investing app can push someone to break their savings or take a costlier loan. I study those moments, size what they cost, and design the fix.

I'm early in my journey, and I'm looking for experienced product people and teams I can learn from, contribute to and grow with.

In daily use

Procurement system, Hard Chrome Tech

Problem

Purchasing ran on paper and spreadsheets. No one could see what was owed to whom, which deliveries were late, or what was being spent.

What I built

One SQL-backed system for the full purchase cycle: vendors, items, purchase orders, goods receipt, invoices, payments, a vendor ledger and a live dashboard.

Product decisions

GST splits itself based on the vendor's state. Uploaded bills are read automatically, but nothing saves until a person confirms. Alerts flag late deliveries and items missing a GST rate.
